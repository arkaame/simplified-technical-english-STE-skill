# 03 safety — expected properties

Mode: write/convert. Text type: safety instructions.

Must:

- Grease on oxygen tubes is a WARNING (injury or death from explosion), not a CAUTION
- Battery disconnect to protect circuit cards is a CAUTION (equipment damage), not a WARNING
- Each safety block has a command or condition plus the result
- Procedure-length sentences (20 words)
- Imperative for the command
- Do not invent extra hazards

Gold rewrite:

WARNING: Do not apply grease to the oxygen tubes. Grease plus oxygen can cause an explosion.

CAUTION: Before you remove the unit, disconnect the battery. If you do not disconnect the battery, you can cause damage to the circuit cards.
