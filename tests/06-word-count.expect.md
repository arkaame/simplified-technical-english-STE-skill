# 06 word count — expected properties

Mode: write/convert. Text type: procedure.

Must:

- Split the long sentence. Procedure limit is 20 words
- Parenthetical counts as one word in the outer sentence and as its own inner sentence
- `EMER` and quoted/legend text stay unchanged
- `In-Flight Entertainment` / IFE style names count as specified in Section 8
- No `accomplish`; no `subsequent` as a run-on modifier if a simpler sequence works
- No invented checkout steps

Gold rewrite:

1. Make sure that the EMER pushbutton switch is released (the EMER legend is off).
2. Do an operational checkout of the in-flight entertainment system.
3. Then do the remaining tasks.
