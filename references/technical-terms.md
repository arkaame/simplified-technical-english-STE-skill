# Technical nouns and technical verbs (Issue 9)

These categories decide whether a word that is not in the STE dictionary may still appear. The listed words are examples from the standard, not a closed list. Prefer a company glossary when the user has one.

If an approved dictionary verb already gives the meaning, do not use a technical verb.

## Technical noun categories (Rule 1.5)

1. Official parts information — bolt, cable, clip, engine, filter, hatch, pipe, propeller, screw, switch, transceiver
2. Vehicles or machines, and locations on them — aircraft, airframe, cabin, cockpit, fuselage, helicopter, ship, wing
3. Tools and support equipment, their parts, and locations — clamp, cover, drill, gauge, jack, label, torque wrench, test rig
4. Materials, consumables, and unwanted material — adhesive, fuel, grease, oil, paint, sealant, stainless steel, tape, water, wire
5. Facilities, infrastructure, and logistic procedures — airport, hangar, dock, shop, storage, transport
6. Systems, components and circuits, functions, configurations, and parts — air conditioning, exhaust, latch, pump, standby mode, vent
7. Mathematical, scientific, engineering terms, and formulas — diameter, pressure, torque, voltage, cycle, defect, temperature
8. Navigation and geographic terms — altitude, heading, pitch, roll, north, landing, glideslope
9. Numbers, units of measurement and time — 92, ampere (A), hour (h), kilogram (kg), meter (m), zero
10. Quoted text — EXIT sign, ON position, FAULT legend, FASTEN SAFETY BELT sign
11. Professional roles, individuals, groups, organizations, geopolitical entities — crew, manufacturer, operator, FAA, EASA
12. Parts of the body — hand, eyes, skin, respiratory tract
13. Common personal effects, food, and beverages — clothing, footwear, coffee
14. Medical terms — asthma, nausea, pulse, medication
15. Official documents, parts of documentation, standards, and guidelines — chapter, figure, warning, caution, note, Service Bulletin, data module
16. Environmental and operational conditions — ice, rain, humidity, lightning, night, turbulence
17. Colors — black, red, yellow, green, white (no comparative or superlative)
18. Damage terms — corrosion, crack, dent, scratch, distortion, fraying
19. Computer science, information and communication technology — file, database, screen, software, network, icon
20. Civil and military operations — mission, deployment, customer support, OEM
21. Law and regulations — contract, compliance, waiver, jurisdiction
22. Animals, plants, and other life forms — bird, bacteria, insect

## Technical verb categories (Rule 1.12)

Use these only in the matching subject field, and only when no approved dictionary verb is accurate.

1. Manufacturing processes
   - Remove material — drill, grind, mill, ream, unsolder
   - Add material — insulate, remetal
   - Attach material — braze, crimp, solder, weld
   - Change strength or properties — anneal, cure, freeze, heat-treat, magnetize
   - Change surface finish — buff, burnish, plate, polish
   - Change shape — cast, extrude, stamp
2. Computer processes and applications
   - Input and output — click, enter, press, print, tap, type
   - User interface — copy, cut, delete, drag, open, paste, save, scroll
   - System operations — boot, download, install, load, reboot, update, upload
3. Instructions in a subject field
   - Engineering / scientific — convert, detect, modulate, transform
   - Medical — disinfect, intubate, sterilize
   - Civil and military — arm, fire, inhibit, load, unload
   - Navigation — descend, fly, hover, land, take off, taxi
   - Automotive and railway — accelerate, brake, park, steer
   - Energy, oil, and gas — compress, distill, drill, inject, pump
4. Law and regulations — comply with, conform to, notify, waive (inspection or requirement)

## Prefer dictionary verb + technical noun

- Not Clamp the cable. Write Put clamps on the cable to hold it in position.
- Not Grease the fasteners. Write Apply grease to the fasteners.
- Not Wire the cable to the structure. Write Attach the cable to the structure with wire.
- Not If you detect broken wires, repair them. Write If you find broken wires, repair them. (detect is a technical verb only when a device detects something.)
