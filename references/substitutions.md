# Frequent replacements (working subset)

This is a writer's aid, not the official ASD-STE100 dictionary. UPPERCASE is the usual approved alternative or construction. If a swap would change meaning, rebuild the sentence (Rule 9.1).

## Function words and style

| Do not write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| don't, can't, it's, you're | DO NOT, CANNOT, IT IS, YOU ARE |
| e.g., i.e., etc., viz. | FOR EXAMPLE, THAT IS, or a full list |
| and/or | AND or OR. If both are true, write AND OR only when the spec requires it, or write two sentences |
| via | THROUGH or WITH |
| keep away from | DO NOT PUT ... ON or DO NOT APPLY |
| upon | ON or AFTER |
| within | IN or BEFORE the limit |
| among | BETWEEN (two) or IN |
| at least | A MINIMUM OF |
| no longer | NOT ... ANYMORE or the MAXIMUM LENGTH construction |
| in case of | IF |
| such as | FOR EXAMPLE |
| he, she, his, her | YOU, THE TECHNICIAN, THE OPERATOR, or repeat the noun |
| etc. at the end of a list | stop the list or write OR OTHER [items] |

## Meaning traps (Rule 1.3 and 9.1)

- JUST — alternative immediately changes meaning when JUST means ONLY. Write ONLY.
- FOLLOW — approved meaning is come after. Instructions are OBEYED.
- CLEAR (v) — often not the meaning you want. Rebuild the sentence.
- WITH — association, help, or instrument. If the sentence can be read three ways, rewrite.
- THAT — keep it after MAKE SURE, SHOW, RECOMMEND.

## Rebuild patterns (Rule 9.1)

Unapproved noun of action → approved verb

- Before the removal of the unit → Before you remove the unit
- After installation of the cover → After you install the cover
- Perform an inspection of the lines → Do an inspection of the lines

Unapproved adjective → clause

- The valve is operable → The valve can operate
- It is possible that leakage will occur → Leakage can occur
- Make sure the unit is functional → Make sure that the unit operates correctly

Long modifier stack → two sentences or a list

- stainless steel corrosion protection strips → strips that give protection against corrosion. These strips are made of stainless steel.
